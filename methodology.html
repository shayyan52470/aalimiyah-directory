Methodology

Useful comparisons, without pretending every programme is the same.

The directory separates published facts, editorial classifications and unverified gaps. Sources are retained so readers can check important details themselves.

01

Programme-level records

Each row describes one programme, not an institution in general. Different formats or tracks receive separate records.

02

Evidence before completeness

An empty field means “not yet verified.” We avoid turning likelihoods or assumptions into facts.

03

Neutral descriptions

The directory records a programme’s stated curriculum and orientation without ranking religious legitimacy or teaching quality.

What we record

The data is organised into five groups. Identity fields distinguish the programme; delivery fields describe how a student participates; academic fields cover curriculum and subjects; cost and recognition fields capture practical claims; provenance fields show where and when those claims were checked.

Identity

institution, programme_name, programme_type, status

Delivery

delivery_mode, location, timezone, languages, gender, study load, duration and schedule

Academic

Arabic entry level, curriculum, jurisprudence and theology, qualification, subjects and named texts

Practical

Entry requirements, accreditation, tuition period and currency, financial aid

Provenance

Official and syllabus URLs, all source URLs, verification date and restrained editorial notes

Subjects are not general tags

subjects contains areas formally taught—for example, Hadith, Fiqh, Arabic grammar or Tafsir. tags is reserved for useful cross-cutting characteristics that do not have their own field, such as dars-nizami, live-classes, recordings-available or self-paced. Values use semicolons and a consistent vocabulary.

Published, draft and archived

Published records have enough information and source material to appear in the main directory. Draft records are research leads visible in the data explorer but excluded from the main results. Archived records preserve programmes that have ended or can no longer be verified.

What we deliberately exclude

Private contact details, personal living preferences, unsourced reputational scores and unattributed opinions are not imported from research sheets. The public CSV is produced from an explicit list of approved columns.

For the exact field definitions and accepted values, see DATA_GUIDE.md on GitHub.
